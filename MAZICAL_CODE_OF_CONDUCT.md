# Mazic Code of Conduct

## Our Magical Pledge

In the mystical realm of Mazic, we, as mages, contributors, and guardians, solemnly pledge to create a welcoming and enchanting experience for all who enter our labyrinth. Regardless of age, appearance, abilities, background, experience level, or magical affinity, we embrace diversity in all its forms.

We vow to cast spells of kindness, respect, and inclusivity, fostering an environment where creativity and collaboration flourish like the most potent of magical artifacts.

## Our Arcane Standards

### Behaviors that Empower Our Magical Community:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness towards fellow mages
* Respecting diverse opinions, experiences, and magical practices
* Offering and graciously receiving constructive feedback
* Taking responsibility for our magical mishaps and learning from them
* Prioritizing the greater good of our magical community

### Behaviors that Disrupt Our Magical Harmony:

* Using language or imagery that demeans or objectifies others
* Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
* Any form of harassment, public or private
* Revealing another mage's personal information without explicit consent
* Conduct unbefitting of a respectable mage in our magical realm

## Enforcement of the Magical Code

The Council of Elder Mages is responsible for maintaining the balance of our magical community. They have the power and duty to address any behavior that breaches this code, from issuing warnings to banishing those who persistently disrupt our harmony.

## Scope of the Enchantment

This Code of Conduct applies within all spaces of our magical realm, including when representing Mazic in the mundane world.

## Consequences of Breaking the Magical Code

### 1. Gentle Correction
**Impact**: Minor magical mishaps or misunderstandings.
**Consequence**: A private message from an Elder Mage, explaining the nature of the violation.

### 2. Warning
**Impact**: Repeated minor violations or a single significant breach.
**Consequence**: A formal warning. The mage may be asked to retreat from certain magical circles for a specified time.

### 3. Temporary Banishment
**Impact**: Serious violation of community standards or persistent inappropriate behavior.
**Consequence**: Temporary banishment from the magical realm for a specified period.

### 4. Permanent Exile
**Impact**: Egregious violations or a pattern of serious misconduct.
**Consequence**: Permanent exile from the Mazic magical community.
